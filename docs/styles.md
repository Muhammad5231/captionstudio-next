# CaptionStudio — 60+ Caption Styles & Kinetic Typography

## Overview
CaptionStudio features over 60 pre-calibrated caption style presets across diverse social and cinematic categories.

## Style Categories
1. **VIRAL_BOLD**: High-impact, heavy-stroke styles tailored for TikTok hooks, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels (e.g., *MrBeast Punch*, *Hormozi Gold*, *TikTok Viral Yellow*).
2. **KINETIC**: Energetic motion presets with elastic pops, bouncy word highlights, and letter tracking animations (e.g., *Hyper Kinetic*, *Dynamic Spring*).
3. **HIGHLIGHT**: Word-by-word karaoke illumination where active words light up with vibrant contrast while upcoming words remain subdued (e.g., *Karaoke Neon*, *Sing-Along Cyan*).
4. **MINIMAL**: Elegant, low-distraction subtitles with clean typography and subtle background blur pills (e.g., *Clean Helvetica*, *Subtle Matte Box*).
5. **CINEMATIC**: Letterbox-safe documentary subtitles with serif or geometric typefaces (e.g., *Classic Cinema*, *Letterbox Drama*).
6. **CREATOR_SOCIAL**: Modern podcast and tech creator aesthetic presets (e.g., *Modern Podcast*, *Coding Glow*).

## Animation Types
- `word-pop`: Active word scales up slightly (1.15x) upon speech onset.
- `bounce`: Playful upward spring animation.
- `karaoke`: Word-by-word color shift as speech progresses.
- `fade`: Smooth alpha fade-in.
- `zoom`: Progressive camera zoom emphasis.
- `none`: Static subtitle display.
